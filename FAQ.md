# ChineseStudy Frequently Asked Questions

[English introduction](README.md) · [中文介绍](README.zh-CN.md)

## What is ChineseStudy?

[ChineseStudy](https://chinesestudy.org/) is a website for reading Chinese classics with original text, pronunciation support, translations, and notes. This repository offers an introduction and practical reading guides.

## Do I need to know classical Chinese already?

You can begin by reading a translation alongside a short passage. Pinyin and notes provide additional support, though unfamiliar vocabulary and classical expression still take time to understand. See the [getting started guide](guides/getting-started.md) for a first session.

## Is classical Chinese the same as modern Mandarin?

Classical texts can use words and sentence patterns differently from modern Mandarin. When an expression is unclear, examine its use in the passage and consult the translation and notes. The [pinyin and translation guide](guides/pinyin-and-translation.md) explains how to use these aids together.

## Which languages are available?

The website has [English](https://chinesestudy.org/en/), [简体中文](https://chinesestudy.org/zh-hans/), and [Spanish](https://chinesestudy.org/es/) entrances. Spanish currently offers selected paths around Confucius, Laozi, and Sun Tzu. Coverage varies across works and languages.

## Where should I start?

For poetry, try [Drinking Alone under the Moon](https://chinesestudy.org/en/books/tangshi-sanbai/004-yue-xia-du-zhuo/). For a philosophical passage, try [Tao Te Ching chapter 1](https://chinesestudy.org/en/books/dao-de-jing/01/). Choose the text that interests you, and give yourself time to reread it.

## Does this repository contain the website source code?

It contains introductions, guides, and links to the reading website. It does not contain the application source code or a complete copy of the website’s texts, translations, and media.

## How can I share a passage?

Share its direct website URL with the work and chapter or poem title. A link gives readers access to the surrounding text and explanation. Linking is separate from permission to reproduce translations, commentary, or images; check the applicable terms before republishing those materials.

## How can I report an error or suggest a guide?

Follow the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md). For a content problem, include the exact page URL, language, expression, and a source supporting the suggested correction.
