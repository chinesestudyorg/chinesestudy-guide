# Reading the Tao Te Ching and the Analects

[Home](../README.md) · [Getting started](getting-started.md)

ChineseStudy offers reading editions of the [Tao Te Ching](https://chinesestudy.org/en/books/dao-de-jing/) and [the Analects](https://chinesestudy.org/en/books/lunyu/). Begin with a question small enough to examine in one passage: how a chapter uses a word, or what a speaker is responding to.

## Follow a term through one chapter

Open [Tao Te Ching chapter 1](https://chinesestudy.org/en/books/dao-de-jing/01/). Read the translation once, then locate the repeated characters 道 and 名 in the original. Examine how each occurrence relates to the surrounding sentence.

Write down the wording used in the translation and what you think it explains. Then consult the notes. If a word could carry several senses, keep the alternatives visible until the passage gives you a reason to prefer one.

The [Laozi reading path](https://chinesestudy.org/en/figures/laozi/) provides another route into the text and its themes. Use it to choose a next passage rather than trying to settle a whole philosophical concept from one chapter.

## Read a conversation in context

Choose a short exchange in [the Analects](https://chinesestudy.org/en/books/lunyu/). Identify the speaker, any question being answered, and the situation described. Compare your summary with the translation and explanation.

For a second reading, ask whether the advice depends on the person or circumstance in the exchange. This helps you examine the passage before using an isolated sentence as a general rule.

The [Confucius reading path](https://chinesestudy.org/en/figures/confucius/) links background and ideas with passages for further reading.

## Compare how the passages work

A compact chapter and a conversation may require different kinds of attention. Compare one feature at a time: an image, a repeated word, a speaker’s answer, or the structure of a claim. Record the passage and your reasoning so you can reconsider the comparison later.

Readers who prefer Chinese explanations can use [道德经](https://chinesestudy.org/zh-hans/books/dao-de-jing/) and [论语](https://chinesestudy.org/zh-hans/books/lunyu/). Selected Spanish paths begin at [the Spanish entrance](https://chinesestudy.org/es/).
