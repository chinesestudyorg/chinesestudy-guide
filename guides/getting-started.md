# Getting Started with Classical Chinese on ChineseStudy

For your first session, choose a passage you can finish reading and revisit. A complete short text gives you something specific to understand, even when individual expressions are unfamiliar.

## Choose a reading task

| Your interest | Start here | Your first task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poetry and imagery | [Li Bai’s Drinking Alone under the Moon](https://chinesestudy.org/en/books/tangshi-sanbai/004-yue-xia-du-zhuo/) | Identify the speaker and the companions he imagines. |
| Language and ideas | [Tao Te Ching chapter 1](https://chinesestudy.org/en/books/dao-de-jing/01/) | Compare how the original and translation treat naming. |
| Learning and conduct | [The Analects](https://chinesestudy.org/en/books/lunyu/) | Choose one exchange and identify the question it addresses. |

These are suggested entry points, not a fixed syllabus. A short philosophical chapter can take longer to understand than a longer narrative.

## Try a 15-minute session

**Minutes 1–3:** read the original once. Notice repeated characters and the boundaries between lines or sentences. Use pinyin where pronunciation would otherwise interrupt you.

**Minutes 4–8:** read the translation. Choose one phrase you want to understand more closely, then find its counterpart in the Chinese. You do not need to settle every unfamiliar word in this session.

**Minutes 9–12:** consult the notes. Write down what the phrase means in this passage and any question the explanation leaves open.

**Minutes 13–15:** reread the original. Summarize the passage in one or two sentences. If the meaning still feels unclear, record that uncertainty rather than forcing a conclusion.

## Make the next session easier

Keep the page URL, the phrase you studied, and your question together in your notes. Return to the same passage before starting a new one; rereading lets you test whether your understanding has changed.

When you want another text, browse the [English library](https://chinesestudy.org/en/) or the [Chinese library](https://chinesestudy.org/zh-hans/). The [Tang poetry guide](tang-poetry.md), [philosophy guide](chinese-philosophy.md), and [pinyin and translation guide](pinyin-and-translation.md) offer more focused reading tasks.
