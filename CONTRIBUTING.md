# Contributing to ChineseStudy Reading Guides

Useful contributions help someone read a passage more accurately or find a suitable text. You can suggest a guide, improve an explanation, or report a broken link or content problem.

## Report a reading problem

Open an issue with:

- The page URL and language.
- The exact expression or link you are concerned about.
- What seems wrong and your proposed correction.
- A source for a factual or translation correction, including edition or page details where relevant.

For a website problem, add the steps needed to reproduce it and your browser or device. Avoid including account details or other private information in a public issue.

## Improve a guide

Keep each guide focused on a concrete reading task. Link to the relevant book or passage on [ChineseStudy](https://chinesestudy.org/) and explain why the reader would open it. Use descriptive link text and keep links between repository files relative.

Distinguish the original wording, a translation, and your interpretation. Cite the source when adding a factual claim. Contributions should use your own wording and material you have permission to share.

For small improvements, open a pull request describing what changed and how it helps the reader. For a substantial new guide, begin with an issue describing the intended reader, text, and reading task.

## Check before submitting

Read your changes in Markdown preview, confirm that links reach the intended pages, and check that the guide’s instructions are clear. Content reports are reviewed before any change is made to the reading website.
